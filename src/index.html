Image and video placeholders for design and development

Make dimension-labeled placeholder assets for wireframes, mockups, prototypes, demos, visual tests, and temporary media. Set a custom size, pick a background color, then download the result.

  • Still images: PNG, JPEG, WebP, and SVG. PNG, WebP, and SVG support transparency; GIF supports binary transparency.
  • Animated and video outputs: GIF, Motion JPEG AVI, ZIP files of numbered JPEG frames, MP4, WebM, MOV, and MKV. MP4, WebM, MOV, and MKV can include an optional generated soundtrack.

The placeholder displays its dimensions. Choose a palette or a custom background; the text color is calculated for contrast against it. Both colors can be picked from the same row: the last swatch is split in two, its large triangle being the background and its small one the text, so whichever of the two you pick the other follows. The indicator under the row reports the measured ratio, says where the pair comes from, and offers a fix button when, and only when, the pair cannot be read. With transparency enabled, visible contrast depends on the background beneath the image. The background can also be one of four animated textures, in which case the contrast is measured against the flat color it is drawn over.

How to make a placeholder

  1. Choose Image or Video.
  2. Enter the width and height in pixels, then choose the colors. Pick one and the other one is computed from it to guarantee contrast; pick both and the indicator under the row is the only thing measuring the pair, until you press fix.
  3. Pick the background: solid, transparent, or one of the four animated textures. Textures are painted into every format except SVG, which stays flat.
  4. On a video, set how fast an animated background moves: from 1× to 3×.
  5. Select an output format. For animated and video outputs, set the duration, frame rate, and optional progress bar or clock.
  6. To give a video a soundtrack, tick Include sound and pick one: a synthesized tango, a test beep, a held tone, or noise. MP4, MOV, WebM, and MKV support it; GIF, AVI, and ZIP stay silent.
  7. Select Download to save the generated file.

Private, browser-based, and available offline

Rendering and encoding happen locally in your browser; your settings and generated files are not uploaded. The online page needs a connection to load, but you can then use it offline. You can also download the self-contained placeholder.html and open it later without a server or internet connection.

Formats and limits

  • Width and height can each be from 1 to 4,096 pixels.
  • Video exports are limited to 3,600 frames. H.264 output may round odd dimensions up to even values.
  • MP4, WebM, MOV, and MKV depend on browser WebCodecs and available encoders. Video export is not supported in Firefox for Android.
  • GIF frame timing is quantized, so its actual frame rate may differ slightly from the selected rate.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?
No. The generator creates and downloads files in your browser.
Can I use the generator without internet?
Yes. Use the page after it has loaded, or download the standalone HTML file to open offline.
Can I create a custom size?
Yes. Enter any width and height from 1 to 4,096 pixels.
Can I make a transparent placeholder?
Yes. PNG, WebP, and SVG support transparency, and GIF supports binary transparency. JPEG and the video containers do not. When transparency is enabled, visible text contrast depends on the background behind the file.
Can the background be animated?
Yes. Besides the flat and transparent backgrounds, four animated textures are available: bokeh with grain, fog, center focus, and rising spheres. They are baked into the file for every canvas-based format, including the video containers. SVG is written as text and stays flat, and the loop of a video is not seamless.
Can I pick a color of my own?
Yes, both of them, and from the same place. The last swatch of the color row, after the nine palettes, opens the color picker, and then shows the pair you have instead of the palette icon. That swatch is painted in two halves: the large triangle opens the picker for the background and the small one for the text. Whichever of the two you pick, the other one is computed from it to guarantee WCAG contrast, and the indicator under the row says which one is following. Pick the other half too and nothing is recomputed any more, so the pair is exported as it is: the indicator then shows the measured ratio with the level it reaches and where the pair comes from, and when the pair cannot be read it adds a fix button that hands the text back to the computed color.
Can I change how fast the background moves?
Yes, on any output with more than one frame. Pick from 1× to 3×, and 2× is the default. Turning Texture movement off holds the background still for the whole clip. A still image is the same picture at any speed, so the control only appears when it would change something.
Can a video placeholder include sound?
Yes. MP4, WebM, MOV, and MKV can include an optional synthesized tango, beep, tone, or noise soundtrack. Audio encoding support varies by browser; GIF, AVI, and ZIP exports are silent.
Why might video export be unavailable?
Video formats require browser support for WebCodecs and the requested encoder, and the encoder is the part most likely to be missing.
