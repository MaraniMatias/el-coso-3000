Image and video placeholders for design and development

Make dimension-labeled placeholder assets for wireframes, mockups, prototypes, demos, visual tests, and temporary media. Set a custom size or choose a preset, pick a background color, then download the result.

  • Still images: PNG, JPEG, WebP, and SVG. PNG, WebP, and SVG can carry a transparent background.
  • Animated and video outputs: GIF, Motion JPEG AVI, ZIP files of numbered JPEG frames, MP4, WebM, MOV, and MKV.

The placeholder displays its dimensions. Choose a palette or custom background; the text color is calculated automatically for readable contrast.

How to make a placeholder

  1. Choose Image or Video.
  2. Enter the width and height in pixels, or select a size preset, then choose a background color.
  3. Select an output format. Enable the transparent background for PNG, WebP, SVG, or GIF. For animated and video outputs, set the duration, frame rate, and optional progress bar or clock.
  4. To give a video an audio track, tick Include sound and choose a test sound. MP4, MOV, WebM, and MKV support it; GIF, AVI, and ZIP stay silent.
  5. Select Download to save the generated file.

Private, browser-based, and available offline

Rendering and encoding happen locally in your browser; your settings and generated files are not uploaded. The online page needs a connection to load, but you can then use it offline. You can also download the self-contained placeholder.html and open it later without a server or internet connection.

Formats and limits

  • Width and height can each be from 1 to 4,096 pixels.
  • Video exports are limited to 3,600 frames. H.264 output may round odd dimensions up to even values.
  • MP4, WebM, MOV, and MKV depend on browser WebCodecs and available encoders. Video export is not supported in Firefox for Android.
  • GIF frame timing is quantized, so its actual frame rate may differ slightly from the selected rate.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?
No. The generator creates and downloads files in your browser.
Can I use the generator without internet?
Yes. Use the page after it has loaded, or download the standalone HTML file to open offline.
Can I create a custom size?
Yes. Enter any width and height from 1 to 4,096 pixels, or start with a preset and edit it.
Why might video export be unavailable?
Video formats require browser support for WebCodecs and the requested encoder. Support varies by browser and device.
